* Semantics

- To define the semantics of Skia, we first introduce an abstract model of images, which Skia
  produces.
- As images are a grid of pixels, we model images as maps from points to colors.
- Shapes like rectangles become sets of points
- we combines shapes and images with blend modes (binary operators over colors)
  operators over colors).
- Every Skia program then produces an image, by combinining Images, Shapes, and blend modes in
  different ways
- In the paper not only define the abstract model, but we also show how to convert actual Skia
  programs into this abstract model
